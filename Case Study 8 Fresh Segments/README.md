# Case Study 8. Fresh Segments

[← Back to main README](../README.md)

Fresh Segments is a digital marketing agency that tracks interest metrics for its clients' customers over time. This case study covers cleansing and joining the raw interest data, then analysing which interests are worth keeping based on how consistently they appear month to month.

**Tables involved:** `interest_metrics`, `interest_map`
